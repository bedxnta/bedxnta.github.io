Privacy by Architecture, Not Just by Policy

Most online image tools require you to upload personal photos, receipts, or confidential design assets to remote cloud servers. PixelPlane was engineered with an absolute zero-trust architecture: your files never leave your device.

The Zero-Upload Technical Guarantee

PixelPlane operates entirely within your browser's local sandbox using the modern HTML5 Canvas API, FileReader, and JavaScript TypedArrays. When you drag an image onto PixelPlane:

🔒

No Network Transmission

Your image is decoded into volatile browser RAM. Not a single byte is transmitted across the internet to any API, server, or cloud storage bucket.

⚡

DevTools Verifiable

You can verify this yourself: open your browser's Developer Tools (F12) > Network tab, process an image, and see that zero outbound HTTP POST/PUT requests occur.

Metadata & EXIF Protection

Smartphones and digital cameras automatically inject rich metadata into every photo you take. This often includes:

  • Exact GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, altitude) pointing directly to your home or office
  • Device serial numbers and unique hardware identifiers
  • Exact capture timestamps, lens configuration, and exposure settings
  • Embedded thumbnail caches of previous uncropped edits

When you use PixelPlane's Clean & Strip EXIF tool or re-export through our compression pipeline, the image pixels are rendered afresh onto an HTML5 canvas element. This mathematically discards all embedded EXIF, IPTC, and XMP metadata chunks, ensuring your shared media is sterile and safe.

What Information Is Stored Locally?

PixelPlane uses browser localStorage strictly for user convenience to remember your visual theme preference:

pixelplane-theme: Set to either "dark" or "light" so your theme preference persists between browser visits. No personal data, session tokens, or image history is ever written to storage.
